The story

A designer’s eye. A revenue leader’s discipline.

Trained inDesign & communication
+
Proven inRevenue & GTM

I earned a BFA in Marketing Design & Communications from Texas State University, and that training never left me. I learned early that how an idea is framed and presented often decides whether it succeeds.

I took that instinct into business sales, spending more than a decade at Verizon building and leading B2B and enterprise teams across Texas and the South. I was named to the Winner’s Circle (top 2%) ten years in a row and to the President’s Cabinet (top 1%) three times, and received the People2People National Award, a company honor nominated by my peers.

I went on to lead sales at Intuit for the QuickBooks business as it moved upmarket, rebuilding how customers entered the funnel, launching a new enterprise offering, and opening new business segments inside an established tech giant.

I’ve scaled long-tenured, established teams and built new ones from scratch, and helped high-growth vertical enterprise SaaS companies through successful private-equity exits. Today I lead North America sales for Elite, an AI-powered legal and finance software platform, as a member of the executive leadership team, selling to the CFOs and CIOs of large law firms.

Across all of it, the pattern is the same. I find where strategy, product, pricing, and selling have drifted apart, connect them again, and make the result simple enough for a team to run. That’s the work I built ForgeCity to do.
